The Pivot

Why I Started Building

In September 2025, Lumen Technologies announced a large-scale reduction in force. My role — along with hundreds of others — was eliminated. It wasn't a performance issue. It was a business decision, and it happened to a lot of good people.

For me, it was also an opportunity I couldn't ignore. Throughout my career — at JPMorgan Chase, at Lumen — I kept finding myself drawn to building things. Not just analyzing data or supporting systems, but actually creating tools that people use. I built dashboards that teams relied on. I wrote SQL pipelines that ran every morning. I watched systems and thought: I could build that.

So when the RIF hit, I didn't wait. I founded Prince Tech LLC — a technology consulting and web development company — and started shipping. A real-time ticket sales system for a live community event. A full membership management portal for an association of 80+ members. A bilingual resume website. Real clients, real systems, real data.

"The RIF didn't slow me down. It pushed me toward what I actually wanted to do."
